# The Short Version

I do not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information from visitors to this blog. I do not maintain a database of visitor identities or use personal information to build marketing profiles.

This blog may include posts on personal reflections, technical projects, and other subjects as my interests evolve. However, because websites rely on hosting providers, infrastructure services, and sometimes third-party content, certain technical information may be processed by external service providers when you visit.

While I do not personally collect visitor data, I cannot guarantee that third-party services do not process information about your visit.

# Hosting and Infrastructure

This website relies on third-party service providers to make its content available, maintain security, and deliver resources to visitors.

These services include:

- **GitHub Pages:** Used for website hosting. GitHub may automatically process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information, and request timestamps, for security, operational, and infrastructure purposes.
- **Cloudflare:** Used for website infrastructure, security, content delivery, and media storage through its services, including Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare may process technical information, including IP addresses, browser details, and connection data, when visitors access the website or retrieve its resources.

I do not personally collect, maintain, or use these technical records to identify visitors or track their browsing activities. The collection, processing, and retention of information by these providers are governed by their respective privacy policies and practices.

For more information, please refer to:

- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

# Third-Party Embedded Content and External Links

Some pages may contain content provided by third-party services, such as YouTube videos, Google Maps, images, or other embedded media.

When you view or interact with embedded content, the third-party provider may receive technical information about your visit, use cookies or similar technologies, and process information according to its own privacy policy. Depending on the service, this may include information about your browser, device, IP address, and interactions with the embedded content.

Where available, privacy-enhanced options may be used to reduce unnecessary data collection. However, I cannot guarantee that third-party embedded content will not process information about your visit.

This blog may also contain links to external websites, software repositories, or external resources. I am not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party platforms reached through outbound links.

# Analytics

This blog uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to monitor aggregate page views and performance data. It is a privacy-first analytics service that uses browser-based beacons rather than cookies, local storage, or device fingerprinting. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not collect or process personal data from visitors. For more information, see the [Cloudflare Web Analytics privacy page](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/).

# Cookies and Local Browser Preferences

This website may use basic browser functionality (such as local storage) to remember certain preferences, such as your preferred appearance or display settings. Where such preferences are stored locally on your device, they remain within your browser and are not intentionally transmitted to me.

Third-party services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their use and management are governed by the respective providers' privacy policies and your browser settings.

# Data Retention and Security

I do not maintain a personal database of visitor information or intentionally retain personal information about visitors.

However, third-party hosting, infrastructure, and embedded content providers may retain technical information in accordance with their own policies, operational requirements, and applicable laws.

Although reasonable efforts may be made to maintain a secure website, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

# Your Privacy Choices

You may manage cookies and other browser storage through your browser's settings.

You may also choose not to interact with third-party embedded content. Please note that disabling certain features or blocking third-party services may affect how some parts of the website function.
